### **📂 Portfolio Project: Fraud Workflow Optimization & Tiered Escalation**

**Project Title:** Reducing "Control Friction": A Risk-Based Approach to Fraud Unblocking

**Business Unit:** Fraud Control & Member Experience

**Regulatory Focus:** APRA CPS 230 (Operational Resilience)

---

#### **1. The Problem: The Fraud Bottleneck**

Currently, the bank uses a "One-Size-Fits-All" escalation path. Whether it is a suspicious $5,000 crypto transfer or a $200 unusual Osko payment by an elderly member, the path is the same:

1. **System** triggers a block.
2. **Member** calls the Contact Centre.
3. **Contact Centre Officer** verifies the member but *cannot* unblock.
4. **Officer** must call the external **Fraud Bureau** or internal **Fraud Team**.
5. **Result:** 60-minute wait times, missed callbacks, and "Flood Risk" in the Contact Centre.

#### **3. The Solution: Tiered Delegated Authority**

I proposed a risk-based matrix that allows the bank to maintain high security for "Crown Jewel" transactions while clearing the backlog for low-risk events.

| Transaction Tier | Risk Level | Example | Action Authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier 1: Low** | Low | Confirmed legitimate activity (e.g., travel, simple Osko). | **Senior Contact Centre Officer** |
| **Tier 2: Medium** | Moderate | Rapid transfers to new payees, elderly "unusual" patterns. | **Fraud Bureau (External)** |
| **Tier 3: High** | High | High-value Crypto, Remote Access Scam indicators. | **Internal Fraud Team (SME)** |

#### **4. GRC Justification (The "Why")**

* **Operational Resilience (CPS 230):** By delegating Tier 1 unblocks, we reduce the dependency on the Fraud Team, ensuring the service remains resilient even during high-volume periods.
* **Risk Appetite:** The bank is currently "over-controlling" low-risk events, which costs more in "Cost to Serve" than the actual fraud prevented in those instances.
* **Fraud Accuracy:** Allows the Internal Fraud Team to focus 100% of their time on **Mule Detection** and **Remote Access Scams** rather than routine administrative unblocking.

### **🔍 Visual Breakdown: Fraud Workflow Optimization**

1. **CURRENT STATE (Top Flow):**
* **The Bottleneck:** Every call, regardless of risk, must pass through a "Mandatory Hand-off" to the Fraud Team or Bureau.
* **The Result:** Because this team is limited in staff and external, a backlog is created (Red Box). This leads to the long wait times (60+ minutes) and missed callbacks you've observed.


2. **PROPOSED STATE (Bottom Flow):**
* **The Decision Point (Yellow Diamond):** Instead of a mandatory hand-off, the Contact Centre Officer assesses the **Risk Tier**.
* **The Efficiency Path (Green Box):** For **Low-Risk** scenarios (e.g., travel-related blocks, simple verification of legitimate member activity), authority is delegated to a **Senior Contact Centre Officer** to unblock immediately. This clears the "Flood Risk" and increases member satisfaction.
* **The Specialist Path (Red Box):** Only **High-Risk** scenarios (e.g., Crypto transfers, rapid Osko movements, remote access indicators) are sent to the **Fraud Team**.
* **The Result:** Experts focus on actual scams (Mule detection) rather than routine admin tasks.
